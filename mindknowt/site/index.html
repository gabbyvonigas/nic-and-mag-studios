One less thing on your mind.

MindKnowt gives all those little things taking up space in your head somewhere else to live. Set a Knowt, place a Knowt Tag where the task lives, and when it's time, one simple tap closes the loop.

Coming soon to the App Store

Meet the Knowt Tag.

A tiny tag with one simple job: helping you follow through without keeping it all in your head.

A Knowt Tag uses NFC technology, a small, battery-free tag your iPhone reads with a tap. No pairing. No charging. Nothing else to keep track of.

How it works

  1. Stick

    Put a Knowt Tag where the task lives.

  2. Set

    Create your Knowt and choose when you want the nudge.

  3. Tap

    When it's time, tap your phone to the Knowt Tag. Done. Logged and closed.
