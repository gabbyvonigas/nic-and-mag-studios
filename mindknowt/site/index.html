A reminder you can only turn off by being there.

Stick an NFC tag where the task actually is. The alarm keeps going until you walk over and scan it, so the thing gets done instead of dismissed from the sofa.

Coming soon to the App Store

How it works

  1. Stick a tag

    Put an NFC tag on the pill box, the fridge, the back door, wherever the task lives.

  2. Point a knowt at it

    Make a reminder, set when it should ring, and attach it to that tag.

  3. Scan to stop it

    When it rings, hold your phone to the tag. Getting there is what turns it off.
