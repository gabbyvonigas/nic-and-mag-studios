Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

MindKnowt is made by Nic & Mag Studios LLC, an Oklahoma limited liability company based in Tulsa, Oklahoma. This policy explains what the app collects and what happens to it. It covers the MindKnowt iPhone app and this website.

What stays on your phone

Your reminders, categories, schedules, tag assignments and completion history are stored in a database on your device. We do not receive them, we cannot read them, and they are not sent anywhere by the app. Deleting the app deletes them.

Sign in with Apple

When account sign-in is available, MindKnowt will offer Sign in with Apple and no other sign-in method. Apple gives us an identifier for you that is specific to this app, and your name and email address if you choose to share them. Apple's Hide My Email option gives us a relay address instead of your real one, which works normally for anything we send you.

We use that identifier to recognize you across your devices and to connect your orders to you. We do not use it for advertising and we do not sell it.

Syncing between your devices

When sync is available, MindKnowt will use Apple's CloudKit to copy your reminders into your own private iCloud storage so they survive a lost or replaced phone. That data lives in your iCloud account, not on our servers. We cannot read it. If you are not signed in to iCloud, sync does not run and the app works exactly as it does now.

Ordering tags

The free starter pack of NFC tags is ordered through a Shopify checkout. The shipping address, name, email address and any payment details are entered on Shopify's checkout page and handled by Shopify, not by the app. We see the resulting order so we can send the tags.

Shopify processes that information as our service provider under its own privacy terms. The app itself no longer collects or stores a postal address anywhere.

Buying more tags

The app links to Amazon for additional tags. That is an Amazon Associates link, which means we earn a commission on qualifying purchases. Anything you do on Amazon is between you and Amazon, under Amazon's own privacy policy.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs in the app.
  • No third-party trackers, cookies or email capture on this website.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.
  • No location collection. The app never asks for your location.

Children

MindKnowt is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

Deleting your account and your data

Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device.

Once accounts exist, you will be able to delete your account from inside the app, under Settings. Deleting your account removes the identifier Apple gave us, any synced reminder data held in your private iCloud storage, and disconnects your order history from you. You can also revoke MindKnowt's access entirely in your iPhone's Settings, under your name, then Sign in with Apple.

If you would rather we did it, email hello@mindknowt.com from the address on your account and we will delete it. We keep order records where tax and accounting rules require it, with the order separated from your account.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected, or to have it deleted. Email us and we will answer. We will not treat you differently for asking.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top and, where the change is significant, say so in the app.
