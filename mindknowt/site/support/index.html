What kind of NFC tags work?

Ordinary NTAG215 stickers, which are the ones we ship and test with. Most NTAG213, NTAG215 and NTAG216 tags work. You do not have to buy tags from us. Tags you already own are fine, as long as your iPhone can read them.

Where should I put a tag?

Wherever the task actually happens: on the pill box, inside a cabinet door, on the back of the front door, on the washing machine. The point is that turning the alarm off means being in the place where you would do the thing anyway.

How do I get the free 5-pack?

Open the app, go to Settings, then "Your first 5 tags". Checkout opens in Safari and you enter your shipping address there. It is one pack per customer, free, and we post it to you.

My tag will not scan.

Hold the top of your iPhone against the tag and keep it still for a second or two. Metal behind a tag blocks it, so a tag stuck directly to a fridge door or a metal cabinet often will not read. Move it to the plastic trim or a door frame instead.

Can I use a reminder without a tag?

Yes. A knowt set to Alarm Only rings and is dismissed like a normal alarm. Tags are for the things you are most likely to turn off and then not do.

How do I delete my account?

Once accounts are available, you can delete yours in the app under Settings, and that removes your account and any synced data. You can also revoke access from your iPhone's Settings, under your name, then Sign in with Apple. If you would rather we handled it, email us from the address on your account. The Privacy Policy sets out exactly what deletion removes.

Will my reminders survive a new phone?

Today everything is stored on your device, so moving to a new phone without a backup means setting your reminders up again. Sync through your own private iCloud storage is being built. We will say so in the app when it is live.
