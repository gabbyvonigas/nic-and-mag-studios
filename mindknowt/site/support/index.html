What kind of tags work?

A Knowt Tag is an ordinary NFC sticker. NTAG213, NTAG215 and NTAG216 all work, and NTAG215 is the one we test with. Tags you already own are fine, as long as your iPhone can read them.

Where should I put a Knowt Tag?

Wherever the task actually happens: on the pill box, inside a cabinet door, on the back of the front door, on the washing machine. Closing a Knowt means being in the place where you would do the thing anyway.

My Knowt Tag will not scan.

Hold the top of your iPhone against the tag and keep it still for a second or two. Metal behind a tag blocks it, so one stuck directly to a fridge door or a metal cabinet often will not read. Move it to the plastic trim or a door frame instead.

Can I use a Knowt without a Knowt Tag?

Yes. A Knowt set to Alarm Only rings and is dismissed like a normal alarm. Knowt Tags are for the things you are most likely to turn off and then not do.

How do I delete my account?

Once accounts are available, you can delete yours in the app under Settings, and that removes your account and any synced data. You can also revoke access from your iPhone's Settings, under your name, then Sign in with Apple. If you would rather we handled it, email us from the address on your account. The Privacy Policy sets out exactly what deletion removes.

Will my Knowts survive a new phone?

Today everything is stored on your device, so moving to a new phone without a backup means setting your Knowts up again. Sync through your own private iCloud storage is being built. We will say so in the app when it is live.
